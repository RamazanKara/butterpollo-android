PyroWave integration for Rubylight Android

Android renderer adapted from joemossjr16/artemis-android-pyrowave
https://github.com/joemossjr16/artemis-android-pyrowave/tree/387d3a5ce1e3df8d4a4d29eec5b81a7b904926a5
GPL-3.0, as is this Moonlight-derived application (see LICENSE.txt).
Rubylight changes: protocol, capability checks, 10-bit/HDR, timing and lifecycle.
